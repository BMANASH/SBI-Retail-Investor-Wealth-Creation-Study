## Executive Summary

Conducted over an 8-week research internship at the **State Bank Institute of Learning and Development (SBILD Guwahati)**, State Bank of India, this study investigates wealth creation behavior, risk tolerance, planning discipline, and digital platform adoption among individual retail investors ($N = 200$) in Guwahati, Assam[cite: 1, 2, 3]. 

This project evaluated how psychological and behavioral traits interact with capital allocation choices[cite: 2]. The empirical findings were subsequently peer-reviewed and published in the ***Journal of Management in Practice* (Vol. 11, No. 1, May 15, 2026, ISSN: 2456-1509)**[cite: 2].

---

## Key Institutional & Analytical Deliverables

* **Primary Empirical Survey ($N = 200$):** Sampled active retail investors across SBI, ICICI, HDFC, and PNB bank branches and digital channels in Guwahati.
* **Statistical Rigor:** Computed Cronbach's Alpha ($\alpha = 0.827$) across a 15-item Likert scale to verify instrument internal consistency[cite: 1, 2].
* **Hypothesis Testing:** Executed Chi-Square tests of independence ($\chi^2$) across 3 core behavioral relationships, controlling for employment status[cite: 1, 2].
* **Diagnostic Insights:** Identified the **Intention-Action Gap** (high strategic belief vs. low structured execution) and the **Advisory Gap** (51.8% relying on family/friends vs. 35.1% professional advice)[cite: 1, 2].

## Statistical Methodology & Mathematical Proofs

### 1. Instrument Internal Consistency — Cronbach's Alpha ($\alpha$)

Evaluated across 15 Likert-scale statements ($k = 15$) assessing investor risk awareness, goal setting, and strategic confidence[cite: 1, 2]:

$$\alpha = \left(\frac{k}{k - 1}\right) \left(1 - \frac{\sum S_i^2}{S_y^2}\right)$$

* **Number of Items ($k$):** $15$[cite: 1, 2]
* **Sum of Item Variances ($\sum S_i^2$):** $10.33772$[cite: 1, 2]
* **Total Score Variance ($S_y^2$):** $45.30416$[cite: 1, 2]

$$\alpha = \left(\frac{15}{14}\right) \left(1 - \frac{10.33772}{45.30416}\right) = 1.071428 \times 0.771815 = \mathbf{0.82695} \approx \mathbf{0.827}$$

*Result:* $\alpha = 0.827$ confirms high statistical reliability ($\ge 0.80$ threshold)[cite: 1, 2].

---

### 2. Hypothesis Testing — Chi-Square Tests of Independence ($\alpha = 0.05, df = 4$)

$$\chi^2 = \sum \frac{(O_i - E_i)^2}{E_i}$$

| Test Set | Variable 1 | Variable 2 | Subgroup | $\chi^2$ | $p$-value | Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Set 1a** | Clear Strategy | Return Satisfaction | Salaried ($n=107$) | $5.10$ | $0.276$ | Fail to Reject $H_0$[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Set 1b** | Clear Strategy | Return Satisfaction | Non-Salaried ($n=84$) | $5.98$ | $0.200$ | Fail to Reject $H_0$[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Set 1c** | Clear Strategy | Return Satisfaction | Combined ($N=191$) | $3.91$ | $0.418$ | Fail to Reject $H_0$[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Set 2** | Strategic Belief | Strategy Possession | Combined ($N=191$) | $2.34$ | $0.673$ | Fail to Reject $H_0$[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Set 3** | Risk Awareness | Balanced Portfolio | Combined ($N=191$) | $8.83$ | $0.065$ | Fail to Reject $H_0$[cite: 1, 2] |

*Key Conclusion:* Strategic belief and risk awareness do not automatically translate into structured planning or balanced asset allocation, confirming an **Intention-Action Gap** in retail wealth creation[cite: 1, 2].

## Actionable Institutional & Product Recommendations

1. **Goal-Based Digital Onboarding:** Financial apps (such as SBI YONO) should replace static product catalogs with step-by-step goal-based journeys (e.g., retirement, home purchase, education)[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Behavioral Nudges:** Implement auto-reminder notifications and SIP walkthroughs to transition occasional investors ($61.8\%$) into disciplined monthly contributors[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Hybrid Advisory Systems:** Bridge the advisory gap by embedding accessible robo-advisory tools inside mobile banking platforms to reduce dependence on unverified informal networks[cite: 1, 2].
